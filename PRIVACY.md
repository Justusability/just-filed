# Just Filed privacy policy

Effective 8 October 2026

Just Filed is a Chrome extension made by Cumulative Consulting Pty Ltd, trading as Justusability ("we"). It helps you put bookmarks in the right folder. This policy explains what it reads, what it keeps and what it shares.

## The short version

Everything Just Filed does happens in your browser, on your computer. It has no server, sends nothing over the internet, and shares nothing with anyone, including us.

## What it reads

- **Your bookmarks and folders**: titles, web addresses, folder names and where each bookmark sits. It needs these to suggest folders, to find folders when you type, and to spot bookmarks that look out of place.
- **The page you are on**: its title and web address, only when you open Just Filed yourself (by clicking its icon or using its keyboard shortcut). It does not read the content of any page, and it does not see the pages you visit at other times.

## What it keeps, and where

All of this is stored on your computer, inside Chrome, and only Just Filed can read it:

- your settings, such as how often it speaks up
- which folders you have filed each website into, so its suggestions improve
- a numeric summary of each bookmark title and folder name, made by the AI model bundled with the extension, so it does not have to reread your library each time
- the bookmarks you chose to keep where they are in Tidy up, so it does not suggest them again

None of this is synced to your other devices or sent anywhere.

## What it shares

Nothing. Just Filed makes no network requests. The AI model that matches pages to folders is bundled with the extension and runs on your computer. There is no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party code.

## Your control

- **Forget what it has learned**: use "Forget" in Just Filed's settings.
- **Remove everything**: uninstall the extension. Chrome deletes all the data listed above.
- Just Filed only moves a bookmark or creates a folder when you choose to, and every change can be undone.

## Children

Just Filed is a general productivity tool. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date. If a future version ever needed to send data off your computer, it would ask you first and explain why.
